A working seller app with Aval inside it.

This is Mizan, an example B2B marketplace. Everything in the phone responds: take an advance, watch the limit fall, then simulate the buyer paying and watch it come back.

  • Start on Invoices and press an advance button.
  • Open the Credit tab to see why the limit is what it is.
  • In Activity, simulate day 45 to see repayment at source.
Academic concept for the FinTech Shark Tank assignment, SP Jain School of Global Management. Seller, platform and all figures are synthetic. No real lending takes place.
